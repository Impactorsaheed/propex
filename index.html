Install PropEx on Your Phone (Web PWA)

Use PropEx like a native app with zero App Store download

🤖 Android & Chrome:

1. Tap the top button "📲 Install on Phone".
2. Or tap browser menu (⋮) → "Install App" / "Add to Home Screen".
3. PropEx opens in full screen with offline support!

🍎 iPhone & Safari:

1. Tap the Share button (box with arrow ↑) at the bottom.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
3. Tap Add in the top right. Done!
